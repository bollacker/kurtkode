# Team meeting notes

How this team writes agendas, notes and minutes, so anyone who missed a meeting can catch up in two minutes.

## Fast path

For a meeting under fifteen minutes with no decisions, three lines are enough: who met, what was discussed, and any
follow-up with an owner. Skip the rest of this guide.

## Steps

1. **Before the meeting, write the agenda.** List each topic as a question the meeting should answer ("Do we move
   the launch to May?"), not as a noun ("Launch"). Put the topic that needs a decision first. Give each topic an
   owner and a time box in minutes. Share it at least a day ahead for meetings with more than four people.
2. **At the start, name a note-taker and a timekeeper.** Rotate both roles weekly; the organizer does neither.
3. **During the meeting, capture decisions, not dialogue.** For each topic write: the question, the options raised,
   the decision (or "no decision"), who decided, and why in one sentence. Don't attribute opinions to people unless
   they ask to be on record.
4. **Capture follow-ups as actions.** Each action has one owner (a person, never a group), a due date, and a verb
   ("Send the revised budget to finance by Friday"). Actions without an owner are dropped at the end of the meeting
   unless someone takes them.
5. **Within a day, publish the notes.** Put them in the team folder under the meeting's date, as
   `YYYY-MM-DD-meeting-name.md`, and post a link in the team channel. Mention the owners of new actions.
6. **At the next meeting, review open actions first.** Mark each done, moved (with a new date and a reason), or
   dropped (with a reason).

## Rules

- **Don't** publish notes that quote someone's exact words on a sensitive topic; summarize the position instead.
- **Don't** list attendees who declined; list who attended and who sent apologies.
- Keep the notes under one page. Link to documents rather than pasting them.
- Use the team's names for projects and teams; avoid private nicknames that newcomers won't know.
- Write dates as `YYYY-MM-DD` and times with a time zone.

## Format

```
# <Meeting name> — <YYYY-MM-DD>
Attended: … · Apologies: … · Notes: … · Time: …

## Decisions
- <Question> → <Decision>. <Why, one sentence>. (Decided by …)

## Actions
- [ ] <Owner>: <verb phrase> — due <YYYY-MM-DD>

## Discussed, no decision
- <Question>: <where it stands, what is needed to decide>
```

## When no one can answer

If you were asked to write notes from a recording or a transcript and nobody can clarify, mark unclear decisions as
"(unclear: …)" rather than guessing, and list them at the top so someone can confirm them.

## Done means

The notes follow the format above, every action has one owner and a date, and the file is in the team folder with
the meeting's date in its name.
